Paper: top88 Fig. 3(a) - Sensitivity

Question
How does spatial regularisation change the stiffest half-MBB beam under a 50% material budget?

Setup
60 by 20 square Q4 plane-stress elements; unit thickness; left-edge horizontal symmetry support; bottom-right vertical roller; downward unit force at the top-left node. E0=1, Emin=1e-9, Poisson ratio 0.3, initial density 0.5, SIMP exponent 3, filter radius 2.4 elements, OC move 0.2. Stop at maximum design-variable change below 0.01; safety cap 200 iterations.

How the function computes
Element variables feed the selected filter, SIMP stiffness, a sparse equilibrium solve, compliance and volume sensitivities, then an optimality-criteria update. Only the compliance direction uses the sensitivity heuristic; the volume gradient stays exact.

Mathematics

Equilibrium determines displacement; lower compliance means greater stiffness for this fixed load.

SIMP penalises intermediate material; the small void stiffness regularises the solve.

Distance weights decrease linearly to zero at the filter radius; rows are truncated at the domain boundary.

Sensitivity filtering changes the search direction, while the physical density equals the design.

Self-adjoint compliance sensitivity uses element strain-energy contractions; no extra adjoint solve.

OC uses bisection on lambda to meet the physical-volume budget; bounds include the 0.2 move limit.

Symbols and units
x: design variables; rho: physical densities; N: element count; u and f: displacement and force; K: assembled stiffness; K_e^0: unit-modulus element stiffness; H: distance weights; d_ij: cell-centre distance; s: row sums; g: exact or sensitivity-filtered compliance direction; v: volume gradient; lambda: volume multiplier; v uses the optimizer's summed-density volume convention; ell and h: lower/upper move-and-box bounds. Normalised reference units.

Research target
Andreassen et al. (2011), Figure 3(a), top panel (60 by 20). Panel location and radius checked against the author-hosted uncorrected manuscript; published values must be checked separately if a numerical figure caption is used.

What is compared
The experiment compares final density fields, stopping count and pre-update printed compliance against the unchanged, SHA-256-identified author top88 code on exactly this setup. The paper-panel comparison is visual; native-code agreement is a separate numerical check.

Limitations
A matching example is evidence for this setup, not global optimality or every pairing. The general MBB preset uses radius 1.5 and is intentionally unchanged. Sensitivity filtering is a heuristic, not the derivative of its identity forward map. Last printed compliance belongs to the design before the last update; fresh final-design compliance is reported separately.
