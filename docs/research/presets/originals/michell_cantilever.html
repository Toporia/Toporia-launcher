Michell Cantilever

Question
A point load carried to a clamped ring: one of the few problems whose optimal layout is known analytically, so it checks that a method finds the right shape.

How the function computes
The current linear elastic model maps the design through its filters/material, solves equilibrium, evaluates the selected responses and their adjoints, and updates variables with the configured updater. The associated research demonstration uses separately declared settings; selecting this preset does not silently substitute that experiment.

Mathematics

Density controls elastic stiffness; the equilibrium solve determines compliance. Physical-volume filtering is differentiated before the update.

The representation and configured filters map variables to the physical field; their adjoints carry sensitivities back.

Symbols and units
K: stiffness; u/F: displacement/load; E: modulus; C: compliance. rho: physical density; z: design variables; R/P: representation/filter maps; J: selected scalar objective; J_R/J_P: Jacobians; superscript T: transpose. In the variant expression a is sharpness, s the frozen scale and i a variant. The displayed equations describe the core model; the exact objective/constraints/filter settings are stated above and explained by their own help topics.

What is compared
Use the research census and per-case reports for native/analytic/figure evidence. This original preset retains its own geometry, loads, parameters and stopping rule.

Limitations
Numerical regularization, mesh resolution, grayness, nonconvex optimization and finite stopping tolerances limit interpretation. A finished run need not meet every constraint or reproduce a paper. These model equations do not replace the selected updater, response and filter definitions. Inspect fresh physical metrics and constraint reports.
