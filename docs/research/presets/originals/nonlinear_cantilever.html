Nonlinear Cantilever

Question
A cantilever with a large tip load, designed with geometrically non-linear elasticity (large displacements, Newton–Raphson with load steps) so the design accounts for how the beam bends.

Setup
Original general-purpose preset: Nonlinear Cantilever. Domain 60.0 by 30.0 by 0.0; mesh resolution 0.5; volume budget 0.4; E0=1.0, Emin=1e-09, nu=0.3. Edges [EdgeConstraint(edge='left', dof='both')]; points []; loads [PointLoad(x=60.0, y=15.0)]; load cases [LoadCase(Fmag=0.03, Fa=270.0, weight=1.0, Fe=0.0)]; holes []; springs []; enforced areas [EnforcedArea(points=[(54.0, 11.0), (60.0, 11.0), (60.0, 19.0), (54.0, 19.0)], kind='full')]. Method q4_nonlinear+mma; cap 60; tolerance 0.01; parameters {}; representation {'type': 'element_density'}; material {'type': 'simp'}; filters [{'type': 'density'}]; objective {'type': 'end_compliance'}; constraints []; schedules []; variants {}.

How the function computes
Uses the current total-Lagrangian, load-stepped material/void blend. A fixed uniform field compares the small-load limit with linear elasticity. The associated research demonstration uses separately declared settings; selecting this preset does not silently substitute that experiment.

Mathematics

Load increments and Newton steps solve geometrically nonlinear equilibrium using the consistent tangent.

The end-load objective uses a tangent adjoint; it is not automatically twice the nonlinear strain energy.

The representation and configured filters map variables to the physical field; their adjoints carry sensitivities back.

Symbols and units
R: force residual; K_T: tangent stiffness; a: adjoint; F/u: external force and end displacement. rho: physical density; z: design variables; R/P: representation/filter maps; J: selected scalar objective; J_R/J_P: Jacobians; superscript T: transpose. In the variant expression a is sharpness, s the frozen scale and i a variant. The displayed equations describe the core model; the exact objective/constraints/filter settings are stated above and explained by their own help topics.

Limitations

These model equations do not replace the selected updater, response and filter definitions. Inspect fresh physical metrics and constraint reports.
