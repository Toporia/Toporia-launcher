Heat Sink

Question
A square plate heated uniformly over its whole area, cooled through one small sink in the middle of an edge: distribute 40 % conductor to keep it as cool as possible.

How the function computes
Uses Q4 conduction with distributed generation and prescribed-temperature sinks. A separate strip has an analytic quadratic temperature profile. The associated research demonstration uses separately declared settings; selecting this preset does not silently substitute that experiment.

Mathematics

A steady conduction solve maps prescribed heat input to temperature; a self-adjoint derivative measures the effect of local conductivity.

The representation and configured filters map variables to the physical field; their adjoints carry sensitivities back.

Symbols and units
K_k: conductivity matrix; T: nodal temperature; q: integrated heat generation/point heat input; C_T: thermal compliance. rho: physical density; z: design variables; R/P: representation/filter maps; J: selected scalar objective; J_R/J_P: Jacobians; superscript T: transpose. In the variant expression a is sharpness, s the frozen scale and i a variant. The displayed equations describe the core model; the exact objective/constraints/filter settings are stated above and explained by their own help topics.
