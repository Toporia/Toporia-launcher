Column Buckling

Question
A 60 x 20 mm column clamped at one end and compressed axially at the other: minimise compliance while the buckling load factor stays above a limit.

How the function computes
Runs the existing Q4 pre-stress and eigenvalue engine, with a separate slender-column Euler check. The associated research demonstration uses separately declared settings; selecting this preset does not silently substitute that experiment.

Mathematics

Density controls elastic stiffness; the equilibrium solve determines compliance. Physical-volume filtering is differentiated before the update.

Buckling constraint: Pre-buckling stress supplies geometric stiffness; positive critical load factors are generalized eigenvalues.

Buckling constraint: Minimizing the inverse-factor norm raises the lowest buckling factors; lambda-star sets the scale and sensitivities include a pre-stress adjoint.

The representation and configured filters map variables to the physical field; their adjoints carry sensitivities back.

Symbols and units
K_sigma: geometric stiffness; lambda: load multiplier; phi: buckling mode; p: inverse-power aggregation. rho: physical density; z: design variables; R/P: representation/filter maps; J: selected scalar objective; J_R/J_P: Jacobians; superscript T: transpose. In the variant expression a is sharpness, s the frozen scale and i a variant. The displayed equations describe the core model; the exact objective/constraints/filter settings are stated above and explained by their own help topics.

Limitations
Toporia uses an inverse-factor p-norm rather than the source's KS aggregation, plus own column geometry/interpolation. It is not a direct topBuck250 port. Linear buckling omits postbuckling and imperfections. These model equations do not replace the selected updater, response and filter definitions. Inspect fresh physical metrics and constraint reports.
