Research: Geometric solid and void length indicators

Question
What does Geometric solid and void length indicators compute, and what evidence supports its current implementation?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 0.4 elements per mm (24 x 8 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Constraints: Minimum feature length (geometric) (phase solid, min_length 16); Minimum feature length (geometric) (phase void, min_length 16).
Method: q4+pymoto_mma; runs all 60 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (default settings).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the saved report records the complete scenario, resolved defaults, field and fresh metrics.

How the function computes
Runs the existing cell-centred geometric constraints and tests straight bars against their calibrated nodal indicator.

Mathematics

A physical cone average and discrete gradient identify small solid/void medial features.

The normalized nodal KS measure is conservative within its configured aggregation gap; it uses a mesh-dependent one-dimensional tolerance calibration.

Symbols and units
H: cone average; G: discrete gradient in minimum-cell-spacing units; c: radius-based scale; a: normalized nodal violations; s: KS sharpness.

Research target
Zhou/Dunning length constraints: exact source panel and projection conventions pending.

What is compared
Independent analytic, assembly, gradient and file/metric checks are separate from source-figure reproduction. Histories, fresh final responses and feasibility are recorded without treating a bounded run as convergence.

Limitations
Own physical-field/KS variant is not the original three-field formulation. Gray fields, boundary stencils and mesh resolution prevent a universal minimum-feature certificate. In this bounded 24 x 8 example both length constraints remain violated and the field stays grey: on a grey field every cell violates the indicator (about 3800 times its tolerance at the uniform start), so MMA barely moves. With a Heaviside projection sharpened to beta 32 and the tolerance tightened from 100 to 1 on a 180 x 60 mm MBB (60 x 20 cells, mmapy MMA), the field becomes black and white, but 1-cell members and holes remain and the indicators stay about 5 times their tolerance; pyMOTO MMA's subproblem then takes about 30 s per step. The function does not yet show its intended behaviour; its edge stencils and KS calibration need checking against Zhou et al.
