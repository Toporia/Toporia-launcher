Research: Geometric solid and void length indicators

Question
What does Geometric solid and void length indicators compute, and what evidence supports its current implementation?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 2 elements per mm (120 x 40 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Constraints: Minimum feature length (geometric) (phase solid, min_length 4, limit 64); Minimum feature length (geometric) (phase void, min_length 4, limit 64).
Method: q4+mmapy_mma; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 600 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 3); then Heaviside projection (beta 1).
Continuation: Multiply by a factor on filters[1].beta (start 1, end 16, factor 2, every 60).
Continuation: Multiply by a factor on constraints[0].limit (start 64, end 1, factor 2, every 40).
Continuation: Multiply by a factor on constraints[1].limit (start 64, end 1, factor 2, every 40).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the saved report records the complete scenario, resolved defaults, field and fresh metrics.

How the function computes
Minimises the MBB compliance with 4 mm solid and void length constraints on a 120 x 40 mesh (8 cells), and tests straight bars against the indicator.

Mathematics

A cone average and its discrete gradient find the medial lines of solid members and holes; there an average below the erosion threshold eta (above 1-eta for a hole) means the feature is too thin. The cone radius makes a bar exactly the requested length wide sit at eta.

The normalized nodal KS measure is conservative within its aggregation gap; the tolerance is the centre-line deficit of a bar 10% thinner than requested.

Symbols and units
H: cone average; G: discrete gradient in minimum-cell-spacing units; c: radius-based scale; eta: erosion threshold (0.75); a: normalized nodal violations; s: KS sharpness.

Research target
Zhou/Dunning length constraints: exact source panel and projection conventions pending.

What is compared
Independent analytic, assembly, gradient and file/metric checks are separate from source-figure reproduction. Histories, fresh final responses and feasibility are recorded without treating a bounded run as convergence.

Limitations
Own physical-field/KS variant, not the original three-field formulation; mesh resolution and grey fields prevent a universal minimum-size certificate. Until 29 October 2026 the indicator penalised every cone average below 1 instead of below the threshold: the inside corners of every junction then violated it, so even a truss of wide members was infeasible and the optimiser kept 1-cell members. The constraints are switched in gradually (tolerance 64 times larger at the start, halved every 40 steps) while a projection sharpens to beta 16 (doubled every 60), as in Zhou et al.; started at full strength on a grey field every cell violates and MMA barely moves. The medial-line detector uses a decay of 0.3 times (R/h)^4: at 1 a short thin member between two solid regions escaped, because the average rises along it. Without the constraints the same problem keeps members 1-2 mm wide.
