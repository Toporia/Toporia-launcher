Research: Saved-field 2D post-processing

Question
What does Saved-field 2D post-processing compute, and what evidence supports its current implementation?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+mma; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (default settings).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the saved report records the complete scenario, resolved defaults, field and fresh metrics.

How the function computes
The experiment reuses one saved field and applies each existing post-processor, retaining its files/metrics and separate figures. GUI Run executes the ordinary companion; choose the named operations in the existing Post-processing panel or use the experiment command.

Mathematics

Select the densest free cells, preserving pinned regions; ties and integer rounding can change the effective cutting level.

Connected-component traversal identifies material not touching supports; corner contact alone is not a connection.

A sampled distance transform and medial measurements estimate feature widths; they are not continuum guarantees.

The percent non-discreteness is zero for binary designs and 100 for uniform half-density.

Marching contours or explicit geometry produce a physical-coordinate SVG outline; source and orientation are declared.

Closed polylines encode physical millimetres for CAD; an outline is not a structural reanalysis.

The optional Chaikin rule cuts polygon corners; the default spline is a different smoothing option. Neither preserves structural performance automatically.

Symbols and units
rho: saved physical field; b: binary mask; N/Ns: total/solid cell count; eta: contour level; P/Q/R/v: outline points; Delta x/y/z: cell spacing.

Research target
Own engineering exports/checks and source-inspired metrics; no single originating research figure for the group.

What is compared
Independent analytic, assembly, gradient and file/metric checks are separate from source-figure reproduction. Histories, fresh final responses and feasibility are recorded without treating a bounded run as convergence.

Limitations
Connectivity/feature estimates, smoothing and exported geometry are separate from physical feasibility. File validity does not establish CAD manufacturability or a paper match.
