Research: Minimum volume with aggregate stress

Question
What does Minimum volume with aggregate stress compute, and what evidence supports its current implementation?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 80% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Material volume (default settings).
Constraints: Peak von Mises stress (limit 3).
Method: q4+pymoto_mma with move 0.05; stops when the design changes by less than 0.005, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (default settings).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the saved report records the complete scenario, resolved defaults, field and fresh metrics.

How the function computes
Exercises the existing minimum-volume objective, separate from the built-in volume budget, with the stress response and an existing multi-constraint updater.

Mathematics

Minimize summed physical material subject to the existing adaptively scaled p-norm relaxed-stress limit.

The exact relaxed peak is recorded separately from the adaptive aggregate; density relaxation is not a full-solid stress certificate.

Symbols and units
sigma_vm: element von Mises stress; sigma*: limit; q: relaxation; p: p-norm exponent; s: adaptive scale; N: cell count.

Research target
Le et al. stress-constrained design: exact numbered example pending.

What is compared
Independent analytic, assembly, gradient and file/metric checks are separate from source-figure reproduction. Histories, fresh final responses and feasibility are recorded without treating a bounded run as convergence.

Limitations
Own MBB loading and relaxed stresses differ from the source benchmark. Minimum volume under a stress limit can collapse: with MMA move 0.2, or with a loose limit (6 here), one step thins a member, the stress explodes and the design vanishes. Inspect the reported peak and feasibility.
