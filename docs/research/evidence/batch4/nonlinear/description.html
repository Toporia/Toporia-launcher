Research: Large-displacement elasticity and end compliance

Question
What does Large-displacement elasticity and end compliance compute, and what evidence supports its current implementation?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 30 mm plate, 0.5 elements per mm (30 x 15 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x and y.
Loads at (60, 15); force 0.03 towards -y (down).
Area forced solid: polygon (54, 11), (60, 11), (60, 19), (54, 19).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 40% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise End compliance (non-linear) (default settings).
Method: q4_nonlinear+mma with steps 10; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 150 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (default settings).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the saved report records the complete scenario, resolved defaults, field and fresh metrics.

How the function computes
Uses the current total-Lagrangian, load-stepped material/void blend. A fixed uniform field compares the small-load limit with linear elasticity.

Mathematics

Load increments and Newton steps solve geometrically nonlinear equilibrium using the consistent tangent.

The end-load objective uses a tangent adjoint; it is not automatically twice the nonlinear strain energy.

Symbols and units
R: force residual; K_T: tangent stiffness; a: adjoint; F/u: external force and end displacement.

Research target
Wang et al./Buhl et al. nonlinear designs: matching source geometry, law and panel pending.

What is compared
Independent analytic, assembly, gradient and file/metric checks are separate from source-figure reproduction. Histories, fresh final responses and feasibility are recorded without treating a bounded run as convergence.

Limitations
The current void blend, material law, load pad and Newton controls are declared variant choices. A failed Newton solve must remain a failure, not an optimization result.
