Research: Reaction-diffusion level-set evolution

Question
How does Reaction-diffusion level-set evolution turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
GUI Run is a bounded optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction. Half-MBB domain 60 by 20 mm, mesh 24 by 8, fraction 0.5, E0=1, Emin=1e-9, nu=0.3, top-left downward unit load, left horizontal symmetry and bottom-right vertical roller. Method levelset_reaction_diffusion; cap 160; tol=0 (cap or optimizer's own stop). Representation {'type': 'element_density'}; parameters {}; constraints []; filters []. Full numerical evidence is separate from this recipe.

How the function computes
Evolves its own level-set representation and volume control; does not use a composable updater or the selected representation/filter chain.

Mathematics

Implicit no-flux diffusion regularizes the cell-centred field; bisection chooses a multiplier on the updated smoothed volume before clipping.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. phi: dimensionless positive-solid field; Delta t: pseudo-time step; ell: diffusion length in mm; Delta: Laplacian; T: topological benefit; lambda: volume multiplier; brackets: cell average.

Research target
Otomori et al. (2015) reaction-diffusion examples: numbered panel/inputs pending.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
Cell-centred backward Euler, cubic Heaviside and bisected volume differ from levelset88's augmented-Lagrangian feedback; source plane-stress tensor is not the circular-hole tensor.
