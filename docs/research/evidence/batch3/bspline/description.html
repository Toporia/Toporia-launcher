Research: B-spline density coefficients

Question
How does B-spline density coefficients turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
GUI Run is a bounded optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction. Half-MBB domain 60 by 20 mm, mesh 30 by 10, fraction 0.5, E0=1, Emin=1e-9, nu=0.3, top-left downward unit load, left horizontal symmetry and bottom-right vertical roller. Method q4+mma; cap 120; tol=0 (cap or optimizer's own stop). Representation {'type': 'bspline', 'n_x': 8, 'n_y': 4, 'degree': 3}; parameters {'move': 0.02}; constraints []; filters [{'type': 'density', 'rmin': 1.5}]. Full numerical evidence is separate from this recipe.

How the function computes
Evaluates a smooth field with 32 coefficients on the finer FE grid, then sends physical gradients back through the separable transpose.

Mathematics

Tensor-product basis evaluation maps the coefficient grid to element centres; the backward map is its transpose.

Partition of unity preserves constants and keeps density inside coefficient bounds.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. Z: coefficient grid; Bx/By: clamped uniform spline basis matrices; degree=3; 8 by 4 coefficients.

Research target
Qian (2013) B-spline density-space examples: numbered panel and original knots pending.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
A controlled coefficient field and independent basis check do not reproduce an optimized paper topology or its knot refinement study.
