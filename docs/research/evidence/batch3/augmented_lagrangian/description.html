Research: Local-stress augmented Lagrangian

Question
How does Local-stress augmented Lagrangian turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Constraints: Local von Mises stress (augmented Lagrangian) (limit 1.5).
Method: q4+augmented_lagrangian; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 1.5).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the full numerical evidence is recorded separately.

How the function computes
Runs Q4 compliance with a local relaxed-stress penalty; MMA handles the volume budget while local multipliers update every inner block.

Mathematics

An inequality penalty acts only when its shifted violation is positive. Its finite-iteration output need not satisfy the constraint.

MMA builds a separable convex reciprocal approximation between moving asymptotes; nonnegative coefficients match local derivatives.

A dual/subproblem solve proposes the next bounded design. The local one-budget variant and external multi-constraint implementations are distinct.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. lambda/mu: one multiplier/penalty per local stress observation; L/U,p/q,r: MMA approximation data.

Research target
Giraldo-Londono-Paulino PolyStress examples: matching mesh, stress relaxation and numbered panel pending.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
Q4 ersatz cells and the current density-relaxed stresses differ from PolyStress polygonal meshes. Inspect the exact stress ratio, not just small motion. The limit is 0.8 x the compliance design's peak (met, at 3 % more compliance); the earlier 0.27 x the peak cannot be met with half the material at a point load.
