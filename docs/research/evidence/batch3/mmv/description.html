Research: MMV superelliptical holes

Question
How does MMV superelliptical holes turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+mma with move 0.02; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Moving morphable voids (2D) (n_x 3, n_y 2); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 1.5).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the full numerical evidence is recorded separately.

How the function computes
Moves a fixed set of five-parameter 2D holes, reusing the MMC Jacobian with complementary signs.

Mathematics

Complement the smooth union: solid remains outside the moving superelliptical holes.

Rotate and translate into each bar's axes, evaluate its superellipse, then smooth its boundary at element centres.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. H: smoothed tanh; b: hole index; x'/y',L/t,epsilon: local superellipse geometry and smoothing width.

Research target
Zhang et al. (2017) 3D MMV: source geometry/panel incompatible with this restricted 2D variant.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
The source uses 3D B-spline void surfaces and DOF removal; this variant cannot reproduce them and cannot nucleate new holes.
