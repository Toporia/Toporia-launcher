Research: Floating projection update

Question
How does Floating projection update turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
GUI Run is a bounded optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction. Half-MBB domain 60 by 20 mm, mesh 30 by 10, fraction 0.5, E0=1, Emin=1e-9, nu=0.3, top-left downward unit load, left horizontal symmetry and bottom-right vertical roller. Method q4+floating_projection; cap 120; tol=0 (cap or optimizer's own stop). Representation {'type': 'element_density'}; parameters {'move': 0.02}; constraints []; filters [{'type': 'density', 'rmin': 1.5}]. Full numerical evidence is separate from this recipe.

How the function computes
Combines a damped ratio step, a floating projection and internal sharpness continuation.

Mathematics

The threshold floats to preserve the trial mass; an outer multiplier targets actual filtered volume. The beta-to-zero limit is identity.

A scalar root imposes mass preservation before subsequent bounds and physical-volume checking.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. x: OC ratio trial; beta: sharpness; eta: mass-preserving threshold.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
Own modular filter ordering and shared move limit; not the author's smooth-boundary reconstruction. A capped run may leave internal continuation unfinished.
