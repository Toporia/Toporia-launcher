Research: Adam moments and volume penalty

Question
How does Adam moments and volume penalty turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+adam with lr 0.01; runs all 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 1.5).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the full numerical evidence is recorded separately.

How the function computes
Runs a deterministic independent moment recurrence check and the actual volume-penalized Q4 optimizer.

Mathematics

An inequality penalty acts only when its shifted violation is positive. Its finite-iteration output need not satisfy the constraint.

First and second moments average the augmented objective direction componentwise.

Bias correction removes zero-initialization bias; the configured learning rate decays and box clipping follows.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. m/v: moments; beta1/beta2: decay factors; a_t: decayed rate; d_t: normalized augmented gradient; lambda/mu: multiplier/penalty.

Research target
Kingma-Ba Algorithm 1; Figure 1 learning curves require the original data/task and are not this MBB diagnostic.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
Algorithm recurrence agreement is not reproduction of machine-learning figures. Toporia's default beta1=0.5, decaying rate, clipping and volume penalty are topology-specific choices.
