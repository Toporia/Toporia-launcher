Research: Neural coordinate density

Question
How does Neural coordinate density turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+adam with lr 0.01; runs all 300 iterations.
Design variables: Neural coordinate field (TOuNN-style) (width 20, depth 2, seed 0); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 1.5).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the full numerical evidence is recorded separately.

How the function computes
Uses two 20-wide tanh layers and sigmoid output, initialized to uniform fraction, with the existing Adam updater.

Mathematics

A seeded coordinate network represents densities; a sigmoid bounds output and every weight/bias is optimized.

Explicit NumPy reverse propagation multiplies physical sensitivities by the network Jacobian; no training data or surrogate is used.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. h0: normalized cell coordinates; h: hidden activations; W/b: weights/biases; d: hidden depth; theta: all weights/biases; sigma: logistic function.

Research target
Chandrasekhar-Suresh TOuNN (2021): architecture/optimization figure alignment pending.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
TOuNN uses batch-normalized LeakyReLU and AMSGrad rather than this tanh/Adam-AL variant. Gray fields, approximate volume and local optima remain possible.
