Research: SiMPL primal and latent steps

Question
How does SiMPL primal and latent steps turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+simpl; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 1.5).
GUI Run is an optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction; the full numerical evidence is recorded separately.

How the function computes
Plots a controlled two-variable instance of the primal/latent construction plus a bounded MBB optimization.

Mathematics

A gradient step and volume correction are straight in latent coordinates and curved in densities; lambda can be zero.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. psi: latent logit; alpha: adaptive step; d-hat/v-hat: max-norm-normalized objective/volume gradients; lambda: budget multiplier.

Research target
Kim et al., arXiv:2411.19421v2, Figure 1 schematic and equations 14-15; own numerical coordinates.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
Schematic equation check only. The source does not specify these plotted coordinates. Toporia normalizes gradients and changes step adaptation, and does not reproduce the reported SiMPL-A/B histories.
