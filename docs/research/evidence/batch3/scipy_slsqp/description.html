Research: SLSQP through SciPy

Question
How does SLSQP through SciPy turn a design and sensitivities into the next material field?

Setup
GUI Run is a bounded optimization companion, not a claimed paper reproduction. Half-MBB domain 60 by 20 mm, mesh 30 by 10, fraction 0.5, E0=1, Emin=1e-9, nu=0.3, top-left downward unit load, left horizontal symmetry and bottom-right vertical roller. Method q4+scipy_slsqp; cap 120; tol=0 (cap or optimizer's own stop). Representation {'type': 'element_density'}; parameters {}; constraints []; filters [{'type': 'density', 'rmin': 1.5}]. Full numerical evidence is separate from this recipe.

How the function computes
The adapter supplies exact model derivatives, bounds and normalized constraints to the existing external optimizer. Displayed callbacks and physics evaluations are counted separately.

Mathematics

Sequential quadratic programming models curvature and linearizes constraints before a safeguarded step.

Symbols and units
z: design variables; rho: physical element density; C or f: objective; g: normalized inequality, feasible when <=0; J: Jacobian; superscript T: transpose; plus: next iterate; ell/h: move-and-box bounds. K: stiffness; u: displacement; F: applied load; N: cell count. Coordinates use the scenario's mm units; density is dimensionless. s: proposed step; d: objective gradient; B/D: curvature model; J: constraint Jacobian; Delta: radius; A: active rows; dagger: pseudoinverse; alpha: flow weights; lambda/mu: AL multiplier/penalty.

Research target
Original optimizer numerical example: identified figure and complete inputs pending; library documentation reviewed separately.

What is compared
The experiment records initial/final fields, pre-update objective histories, fresh final compliance, actual physical volume and solve counts. Independent operator checks and source reviews are reported separately. All unmatched paper figures remain in to-fix.

Limitations
The coarse MBB companion checks adapter execution, not the source optimizer's paper figure or global optimality. Small steps, library success and feasibility are separate observations.
