Research: SIMP and HS envelope

Question
What does SIMP and HS envelope compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'hashin_shtrikman'}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Recreate the constitutive curves of Bendsoe-Sigmund Figure 2 at nu=1/3; independently test the rational envelope and SIMP curves.

Mathematics

The 2-D fixed-Poisson elastic ray has this solid-void upper envelope; Figure 2 compares it with rho squared and cubed.

The implemented general envelope uses the stricter bulk/shear bound and binds dimension and Poisson ratio from the scenario.

Symbols and units
rho: physical density; E/E0/Emin: effective/solid/soft moduli; nu: fixed Poisson ratio; q: stricter bulk/shear penalty. The source curve uses normalized E0=1, Emin=0, nu=1/3. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Bendsoe-Sigmund (1999), Figure 2, constitutive curves only.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
The plot does not reconstruct the inset microstructures or solve inverse homogenization. The optimization companion uses Emin=1e-9 for a nonsingular state solve; the curve experiment uses the exact void limit.
