Research: PDE filter: top82 Fig. 4(b)

Question
What does PDE filter: top82 Fig. 4(b) compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'pde', 'rmin': 6.0}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 2.5; maximum 800 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Factorize the nodal filter matrix once and reuse it for forward and adjoint operations.

Mathematics

Helmholtz smoothing with no boundary flux.

Transfer element values to nodes, solve, and transfer back.

Here the discrete filter F is symmetric; the same solve is the exact adjoint.

Symbols and units
x: input cell densities; rho-bar: smoothed field; T: element-to-node averaging; K_f/M_f: filter stiffness/mass matrices; r: PDE length; rmin: radius in cells; F: discrete filter; J: scalar response. Zero normal derivative imposes no flux. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Andreassen et al. (2011), Figure 4, middle bottom density-filter panel, 150 by 50 cells, radius 6.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
The corrected author top82 refuses radii below 2.5; this recipe uses 6. Printed compliance is pre-update. Final-field agreement is checked after one declared vertical row conversion.
