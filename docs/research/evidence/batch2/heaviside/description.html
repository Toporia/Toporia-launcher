Research: Heaviside projection curves

Question
What does Heaviside projection curves compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'heaviside', 'beta': 8.0, 'eta': 0.5, 'beta_max': 8.0}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Plot the implemented tanh projection at fixed thresholds and sharpness values; compare to Wang equation (9).

Mathematics

Threshold eta controls the transition; beta controls sharpness.

The pointwise derivative carries sensitivities back.

Symbols and units
x: input density; P(x): projected density; beta: sharpness; eta: threshold; P prime: derivative with respect to x. All are dimensionless. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Wang et al. (2011), equation (9); Figure 5(f) is a separate pattern-based target.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
The exact Figure 5 design bitmap is not available here. The transfer curves check the published equation, but are not a reproduction of its pattern panel or evidence of minimum length-scale control.
