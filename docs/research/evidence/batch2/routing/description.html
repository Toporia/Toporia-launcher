Research: Router-radius closing

Question
What does Router-radius closing compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+oc; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 2.4); then Routing radius filter (radius_mm 2, P 20, start_iter 0, ramp_iters 1).
GUI Run is an optimization companion with the setup above; it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment. Figure settings: radius_mm 2, P 20, start_iter 0, ramp_iters 1; the reproduction command saves the diagnostic field or curve with all parameters.

How the function computes
Show narrow and wide void channels before/after the current smooth closing, with the threshold and ramp made explicit.

Mathematics

A ramp blends closing only in the interior mask chi; the boundary band passes through.

The closing path first zeros sub-threshold values; the direct blend retains the original x.

The normalized p-norm and complementary erosion differ from the exponential morphology filter; a low-density threshold is applied first.

Symbols and units
x: source density; x-tilde: thresholded closing input; tau: low-density threshold; chi: interior mask; D/E: normalized p-mean dilation and complementary erosion; P: sharpness exponent; n_i: valid stencil count; alpha: ramp fraction; m: cells per mm; r_mm/r_el: tool radius in mm/cells. The boundary band bypasses closing. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Inspired by Sigmund morphology; no published figure for this Toporia-specific router rule.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
A p-norm construction and threshold differ from the paper's exponential operators. A boundary band bypasses the operation. The volume backward pass floors sensitivity at 1e-6 for OC stability and is not the exact volume derivative where that floor is active. It is not a finite-tool accessibility or collision certificate.
