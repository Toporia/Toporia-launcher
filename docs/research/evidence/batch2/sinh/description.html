Research: SINH effective-cost curves

Question
What does SINH effective-cost curves compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'sinh', 'penal': 3.1}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Recreate Bruns Figure 4: the linear first measure and second measures for p=1.1,2.1,3.1,4.1,5.1.

Mathematics

Stiffness is linear; intermediate density is penalized through effective material cost.

The volume adjoint uses this slope, not one.

Symbols and units
rho: physical density; eta1: stiffness measure; eta2: effective resource cost; p: penalty; eta2 prime: derivative of resource cost. Effective cost differs from true geometric volume except at 0/1. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Bruns (2005), Figure 4, equations (22)-(23).

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
A constitutive/cost-curve reproduction does not reproduce the separate resource-filter case-6 topology. Toporia's companion optimization shares one filtered field between both measures.
