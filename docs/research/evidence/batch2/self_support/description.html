Research: Gaynor-Guest support

Question
What does Gaynor-Guest support compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'self_support', 'direction': '+y', 'rmin': 2.4, 'threshold': 0.1, 'beta_support': 20.0, 'beta_local': 35.0}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Show lower-wedge support gating on supported/floating features and independently check the recursive adjoint.

Mathematics

Supported design propagates from the build plate through the lower wedge.

The support gate uses threshold T and support sharpness b; the first layer is supported by the build plate.

A local average and exponential projection produce density; the adjoint reverses both stages and the recursion.

Symbols and units
psi: input design; phi: supported field; S_i: lower support wedge; H_T: tanh gate; T: support threshold; b: support sharpness; F_cone: local cone average; mu: averaged supported field; beta: local projection sharpness; rho: physical density. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Gaynor-Guest (2016), support projection; exact nodal paper panel not reproduced.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
Toporia uses cell centres and a grid-dependent threshold; the paper's nodal examples and this controlled pattern differ. A smooth supported field does not certify a binary design, exact angle or material budget.
