Research: AM overhang filter

Question
What does AM overhang filter compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+oc; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 2.4); then AM overhang filter (direction 0, overhang_angle 45).
GUI Run is an optimization companion with the setup above; it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment. Figure settings: direction 0, overhang_angle 45; the reproduction command saves the diagnostic field or curve with all parameters.

How the function computes
Filter a supported pillar, overhang and floating island; record the build-direction convention and test the complete adjoint.

Mathematics

A smooth support measure uses the preceding layer.

The implementation also uses tiny shifts/backshifts to regularize exact zeros; Ns is the support-stencil size.

A smooth minimum limits printable density; the adjoint follows dependencies through every layer.

Symbols and units
x_i: blueprint density; rho_i: printed density; s_i: support measure; S_i: supporting cells below; Ns: stencil size; P/Q: support exponents; xi0: normalization density; epsilon=1e-4: smooth-min parameter. Tiny regularizing shifts/backshifts are retained in the implementation. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Langelaar (2017), illustrative printing/filter examples; exact panel and geometry require source alignment.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
The field is a controlled diagnostic, not an optimized paper topology or a printability certificate. Angle stencils are discrete and display direction must be checked explicitly.
