Research: Volume-preserving projection

Question
What does Volume-preserving projection compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+mma with move 0.1; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 2.4); then Heaviside projection (beta 16, beta_max 16, eta_mode volume_preserving).
GUI Run is an optimization companion with the setup above; it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment. Figure settings: beta 16, beta_max 16, eta_mode volume_preserving; the reproduction command saves the diagnostic field or curve with all parameters.

How the function computes
Compare fixed-threshold and volume-preserving projection on a nonuniform field and verify the implicit adjoint.

Mathematics

Bisection finds a threshold that preserves the input volume.

The threshold depends on every input; its derivative must be included.

The exact adjoint contains a global correction.

Symbols and units
x_j: input cell value; eta(x): global threshold; P_x and P_eta: partial derivatives of projection; s_j: incoming sensitivity; g_j: input sensitivity. The implicit derivative needs a nonzero sum of P_eta. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Ferrari-Sigmund (2020) volume-preserving construction; exact source figure remains to select.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
Mean preservation does not make every cell or optimized topology agree with top99neo. The input field here is a controlled ramp, not a paper design.
