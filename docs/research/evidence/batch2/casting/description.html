Research: Casting draw direction

Question
What does Casting draw direction compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'casting', 'direction': '+y'}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Show the effect of draw direction on a controlled field; measure the smooth monotonicity bound.

Mathematics

A cumulative smooth minimum propagates material from the parting plane.

Reverse accumulation supplies the exact adjoint of the current recursion.

Symbols and units
x_k: input density along a draw line; rho_k: output; k=0: parting-plane cell; smin: corrected smooth minimum; epsilon=1e-6. The reverse sweep differentiates all preceding dependencies. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Gersborg-Andreasen (2011), casting parameterization; exact formulation differs.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
The cited paper uses a Heaviside parameterization. This smooth running-minimum variant is not a direct reproduction; its output is only approximately monotone and does not model a full mould.
