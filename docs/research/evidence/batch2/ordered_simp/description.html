Research: Ordered SIMP: formula audit

Question
What does Ordered SIMP: formula audit compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'ordered_simp', 'n_materials': 4, 'rho2': 0.4, 'rho3': 0.6, 'E2': 0.5, 'E3': 0.7, 'penal': 3.0}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Recreate the modified curves in da Silveira-Palma Figure 1 at p=1,3,5 and overlay the original formula to expose the attribution difference.

Mathematics

Toporia uses interval-local penalization: the increasing-material branch of the 2022 modified formulation.

The original 2017 expression scales the global power; it is generally different above the first interval.

Symbols and units
rho: normalized density; rho_i: candidate material densities; f_i: candidate modulus fractions; t: local interval coordinate; p: penalty; f: interpolated modulus fraction; A_i/B_i: original global-power coefficients. This implementation requires increasing density and nondecreasing moduli. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
da Silveira-Palma (2022), Figure 1 modified curves, Table 1; compare original equations (2)-(3) separately.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
This verifies stiffness interpolation only, with 1e-8 absolute tolerance for the finite soft phase. No cost interpolation, non-monotone moduli, optimizer trajectory or paper bridge design is claimed.
