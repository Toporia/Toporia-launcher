Research: Constant cross-section

Question
What does Constant cross-section compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Figure settings: {'type': 'extrusion', 'axis': 'y'}. The diagnostic field/curve and all parameters are saved by the reproduction command. GUI Run is an optimization companion on the half-MBB beam (60 by 20 domain; mesh resolution 1.0; maximum 200 steps; volume budget 0.5); it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment.

How the function computes
Demonstrate constant cross-section and check the averaging projection without claiming an optimized 3-D paper shape.

Mathematics

Average each vertical column and broadcast it back; in 3-D choose an extrusion axis.

Symbols and units
P: average-and-broadcast operator; x_kj: input cell density; ny: number of cells along y; i/j: output y/x cell index. All output rows share one cross-section. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Ishii-Aomura (2004), constant-cross-section design; specific optimized panel not reconstructed.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
The diagnostic is 2-D. Reproducing a 3-D optimized paper figure also requires its loads, geometry and optimizer settings.
