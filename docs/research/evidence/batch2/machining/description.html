Research: PDE machining shadows

Question
What does PDE machining shadows compute, and which part of its research claim can be reproduced?

Setup
Domain: 60 x 20 mm plate, 1 element per mm (60 x 20 elements).
Supports: left edge fixed in x; point (60, 0) fixed in y.
Loads at (0, 20); force 1 towards -y (down).
Material: Young's modulus 1 for solid, 1e-09 for void, Poisson ratio 0.3.
Volume budget: at most 50% of the domain may be material.
Objective: minimise Compliance (default settings).
Method: q4+mma with move 0.01; stops when the design changes by less than 0.01, at most 300 iterations.
Design variables: Element densities (default settings); material law: SIMP (power law) (default settings).
Filters, in order: Density filter (rmin 2.4); then PDE machining (axis directions) (from_pos_y True, peclet 1e+04, beta 8, eta 0.4, power -4).
GUI Run is an optimization companion with the setup above; it does not launch the transfer-curve experiment. Figure settings: from_pos_y True, peclet 1e+04, beta 8, eta 0.4, power -4; the reproduction command saves the diagnostic field or curve with all parameters.

How the function computes
Visualize one-sided Cartesian tool shadows and check the full transpose-solve adjoint.

Mathematics

An advection-diffusion solve casts a material shadow from each tool side.

A negative-power mean intersects shadows before bounded projection.

Symbols and units
u: tool-direction velocity; D: diffusion coefficient set by Peclet number; h: mean cell size; T_a: shadow from direction a; T_star: combined shadow; p<0: intersection exponent; n: direction count. Beta and eta control the final bounded projection. Superscript T denotes transpose where used.

Research target
Hoghoj-Traff (2022), advection-diffusion machining; paper geometry is not reproduced by the diagnostic.

What is compared
The report separates independent numerical checks, equation/curve agreement, paper-visual comparison and unresolved reproduction. Diagnostic success alone never sets a paper match.

Limitations
Independent Cartesian cell-centred variant: no finite cutter/holder, oblique directions or forced-void regions. Projection and diffusion can leave access artifacts; no machinability certificate.
